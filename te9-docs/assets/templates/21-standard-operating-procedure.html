Section 1

Purpose

This procedure ensures that every figure in every te9-docs document renders identically: offline, in the house grammar, painted from Clay tokens alone. It turns the suite's recorded decisions — diagrams rendered through pele at build time (ADR-0005, tightened by ADR-0007's pele-strict rule) and the Clay design system (ADR-0002) — into numbered clauses a builder follows and a reviewer checks. Where other templates show, this one governs.

Section 2

Scope

The clause set binds every figure-bearing document the suite produces — this document included: its FIG · 01 was rendered under clauses 4.1–4.7.

Applies to All figure-bearing documents — every template whose format includes a figure plate, from the founding trio's journey chart to this document's own clause chain.
Excludes Hand-authored figures — the sanctioned exception: permitted only when pele cannot express the diagram type at all, and any such exception MUST be argued and noted at the review gate.
Section 3

Prerequisites

Four things must be in place before clause 4 runs. All of them are build-time facts of the skill — a finished document carries none of them.

IDRequirement
PR-1Node.js — a build-time dependency of the skill, never of a finished document.
PR-2pele installed in scripts/ — cd scripts && npm install (pele 1.12.0).
PR-3Figure sources in assets/figures/ — one NN-figNN.mmd per figure, its .spec.json beside it.
PR-4Clay tokens available in the document — the :root token block the pele→Clay variable mapping reads.
Section 4

Procedure

Seven clauses, executed in order. Each is one or more complete sentences; MUST and SHALL mark what the review gate will hold the build to.

Clause 4.1

The author SHALL write the diagram as Mermaid source in assets/figures/NN-figNN.mmd.

Clause 4.2

The author MUST render with scripts/render-figure.mjs, passing --max-width 880.

Clause 4.3

The author SHALL run scripts/clayify.mjs with the .spec.json.

Clause 4.4

The rendered SVG MUST be inlined into the mandatory plate (header / legend / body / footer).

Clause 4.5

Colors MUST come from the pele→Clay variable mapping; raw hex outside the token set is prohibited.

Clause 4.6

The author SHALL verify in the live browser (never headless captures — see research report F3).

Clause 4.7

Figure sources SHALL be kept with the document's figure sources, in assets/figures/.

Drawn, the same seven clauses are one chain: a ring per clause, top to bottom in execution order. This figure is its own compliance proof — authored under 4.1, rendered under 4.2 at --max-width 880, clayified under 4.3, inlined under 4.4 in the plate above, painted from tokens under 4.5, verified live under 4.6, and filed with its sources under 4.7.

Section 5

Verification

Checked at the review gate — the browser approval every suite build passes. A check that fails stops the build; nothing registers forward.

CheckPass condition
V-1 Labels paintEvery weight-600 mono label present in the live browser — headless captures are not evidence (research report, finding F3).
V-2 No collisionsclayify's collision-safe canvas holds: no label overhang clipped at the viewBox edge, nothing colliding with the legend band.
V-3 Dark flipUnder prefers-color-scheme: dark every token flips and the figure still paints — the SVG carries its own dark rules for the fullscreen view.
V-4 PrintLight palette on paper; clause rows, tables and the figure plate do not split across pages; the index strip is hidden.
V-5 Zero network requestsDevTools open on file://: system fonts only, zero requests — the offline contract holds.
