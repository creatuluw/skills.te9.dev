Section 1

Background

te9-docs is a document-suite skill: it produces polished, shareable, single-file HTML documents for human readers. At suite version 2.0.0-rc1 it ships 22 templates in one design system — Clay Explainer — where every document self-describes its own construction, runs on system fonts alone, and carries zero scripts beyond one whitelisted fullscreen listener. The suite already checks itself: contrast guardrails were computed into the design system at design time, and every build passes a browser review gate. But those checks are the author grading its own work. Before the suite is declared 1.0.0, te9-docs wants an independent WCAG 2.2 AA and print-output audit of the whole suite — which is what this RFP commissions.

Suite version2.0.0-rc1 — release candidate; 22 templates on disk of the 35-type launch taxonomy.
Design systemClay Explainer — one shared palette, three system faces, zero webfonts, zero network requests.
DisciplineZero scripts by default; <details> folds and :target jumps instead; one sanctioned fullscreen listener per figure plate.
In-house checksContrast guardrails computed into the style reference (all text tones ≥ 4.5:1 on their grounds) and a browser review gate on every build.
OutstandingAn independent WCAG 2.2 AA audit — light and dark — plus a print-output audit, ahead of v1.0.0.
Section 2

Objectives

Three objectives, numbered. The proposal MUST state how each is met; the report is accepted only when all three are discharged.

Objective 2.1

An independent WCAG 2.2 AA conformance audit of all 22 templates, assessed against the WCAG 2.2 level AA success criteria — light and dark modes audited as separate passes — with every finding graded for severity.

Objective 2.2

A print-output audit of every template at A4 against the suite's print specification: all folds forced open on paper, the light palette printed regardless of OS theme, no component split across a page break, and pointer-era effects absent.

Objective 2.3

A remediation plan that severity-grades every finding, locates it precisely — template file, affected region, and the design token or rule involved — and states the concrete fix, ordered so the suite can land it before v1.0.0.

Section 3

Scope of work

The audit covers the suite as published at 2.0.0-rc1. Work outside the in-scope table is out of contract; anything else requires a written change note under term 8.1.

In scope

IDWork item
S-1All 22 templates at 2.0.0-rc1, plus the template gallery and the compendium of archetypes as published — the full document surface.
S-2Light and dark: every template assessed in both color schemes (prefers-color-scheme), each as its own pass.
S-3Print: A4 print preview per template — pagination, forced-open folds, light palette on paper, break behavior.
S-4Keyboard-only pass: every interactive element — index pills, folds, figure fullscreen buttons — reached and operated without a pointer.

Out of scope

IDExclusion
X-1Redesign — no visual redesign of the Clay design system; findings are fixed within it, not against it.
X-2New templates — the launch taxonomy's pending types are not designed or built under this RFP.
X-3Content edits — placeholder copy is out of scope unless a finding requires a wording change to conform.
X-4The parked step-player experiment (assets/experiments/05-concept-breakdown-v2.html) — parked work is not audited.

Section 5

Section 8

Terms & conditions

Four terms govern any award. They attach to the RFP at issue; a proposal filed to the tracker is proposed under them.

Term 8.1

Fixed fee. The awarded fee is fixed for the full scope of work in section 3: no day rates, and no scope growth without a written change note agreed by both parties before the extra work starts.

Term 8.2

Intellectual property. All audit reports, findings files and remediation artifacts produced under this RFP assign to te9 on payment of the fixed fee. The supplier retains its own pre-existing tooling.

Term 8.3

Confidentiality. Suite materials shared for the audit are confidential. The M-5 NDA is signed before any material changes hands, and the obligation survives the award decision — including for suppliers not awarded.

Term 8.4

Right to reject. This RFP is an invitation to propose, not an offer. te9 MAY reject any or all proposals — including all of them — without stating reasons. Published Q&A answers form part of these terms; a moved date is announced through the tracker before the affected deadline.
