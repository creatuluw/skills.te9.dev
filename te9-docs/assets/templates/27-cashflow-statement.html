Cashflow Statement

The v1.0.0 effort cashflow

Who this is for: whoever holds the build budget next, and the gate after that. This is the suite's build economy stated as a cashflow statement: what was released into the program, what the operating work drew out, the net of each period, and the balance carried forward — denominated in effort hours, not currency. Inflows group above outflows; every period nets; the statement ends, as statements must, under a double rule.

Model · not measurement

The forecast column is the rolling forecast's effort model restated as flows — ≈1 h builds, ≈0.3 h figure ops and ≈15 min of review per gated template, plus the ≈2 h release step. The past is not estimated: P0's ≈13 h is the founding day's own count, and P1's zeros count a folded period, honestly. This document is an accounting grammar, not an audit.

The periods

Three periods, one denomination

A cashflow statement reads periods, not tasks: budget released into each window, operating work drawing it out, the difference netted, the balance carried forward. This one runs the v1.0.0 program across three periods and states its unit before any number — the figures are effort hours, because that is what the suite budgets in.

Statement periods · baseline v2.4.0 · 2026-10-30

Three periods, one denomination — hours, not currency.

P0 · Founding day — one draw, spent to the line: the design system, the workflow, and every template through suite v2.2.0, built inside a single ≈13 h day. P1 · Clusters (to date) — the cluster period: real gates closed, but zero separate hours drawn, because the founding day's carry absorbed the work. P2 · Remaining (forecast) — the window this statement prices from its own gate: the eight formats & remaining builds and the v1.0.0 release step, against the ≈15 h envelope the rolling forecast left open. The unit is deliberate — the suite has no money line; note 01 in the accounting policy says what would change if it ever did.

Where P0's hardening sits — the ≈2 h that never drew a line
The folding note — why the founding day's outflow is ≈13.0, not ≈15.0
  • The day drew one budget. P0 released ≈13.0 h and drew ≈13.0: the statement's P0 lines — ≈11.0 builds, ≈2.0 figure ops — decompose that one draw; they are not four separate withdrawals.
  • The hardening rode inside the build hours. The figure pipeline's label-rendering repair (edge-label chips painting black), the alpha pass on the tints, and the base-reading-size doctrine each cost real minutes — all inside the ≈11.0 build line, so the fixes row carries no separate P0 figure.
  • The grammar still shows them. The row exists, marked —, so a reader knows folding happened on purpose; the alternative — silently inflating the row to ≈2.0 against a ≈13.0 release — would break the balance this statement exists to keep.

Accounting policy

Five notes, small

The policies that make the statement readable — each one load-bearing; the statement is false without them. They are kept small and in the open, like policy should be.

Note 01 · Denomination
Effort hours, not currency. The suite has no money line; every figure here is build effort. The accounting grammar — inflows above outflows, net per period, the double-ruled close — is indifferent to the unit: it would survive re-denomination intact.
Note 02 · Estimates vs actuals
Model · not measurement — P2 is the rolling forecast's effort model restated as flows, and every one of its numerals carries ≈ at point of use. P0 is counted history at the day's own resolution. No invented precision: bands stay bands, and the band's ends show their arithmetic.
Note 03 · The closing band
P2 closes between ≈0.0 and ≈2.0 h — the model plans the program at near-zero float by design. Unspent hours are not "returned": hours are not money; the envelope simply expires at the gate. The double rule closes the statement, it does not promise the top of the band.
Note 04 · The re-roll clause
The rolling forecast's re-roll rule re-baselines P2 at each cluster completion — actuals reprice the rates, the window slides, the closing band re-derives. This statement is one baseline's snapshot (v2.4.0); at the next gate it is superseded, not defended.
Note 05 · Outside the model
The financial pair's own build hours — this statement and the rolling forecast do not bill themselves; folded like every cluster's internal overhead. The RFI-2026-01 chain's process effort stays unpriced too, exactly as in the forecast.
What a currency budget would change — the re-denomination clause
Note 01, unfolded — the unit changes, the grammar does not
  • What flips: releases become currency lines, the per-template rates become burn, the closing band becomes runway — and the ≈ signs still mark every estimate, because a forecast is a forecast in any unit.
  • What survives: the grouping (inflows above outflows), the folded-vs-drawn distinction, the single rule above totals, the double rule under the close. The double rule does not care what it rules.
  • What would be lost: nothing that matters — and gained: a statement a finance reader can sign. That is the whole point of building the grammar in hours first.
