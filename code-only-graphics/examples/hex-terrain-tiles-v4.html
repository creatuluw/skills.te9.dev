No more tile prisms: the board is one continuous landscape. Each family's elevation spreads from its centre as a smooth dome, so every tile meets its neighbours on a natural slope, all around. The v3 adaptive-neighbour engine still decides everything: roads continue and fade by terrain affinity, the mountain river rides the dome downhill and pours into the marsh bog, forests/sand/snow bleed across borders. Rivers drape over the real surface and drop where it's steep.
