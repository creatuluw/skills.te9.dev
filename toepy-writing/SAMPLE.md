# Want to see what your data is NOT telling you?

This post will help you to understand a simple trick I have been using for years
to find the blind spots in my own dashboards.

A while back I built a running dashboard for myself. I tracked everything:
distance, pace, heartbeat. It looked really nice and I was proud of it. But after
two weeks I stopped using it. Not because it was broken, but because it only
showed me what I already knew. It was like checking the weather by looking
outside. A no brainer, I know.

That is when I started adding the things I could not see:

1. Add an "excluded" panel to your sheet

   a. Use a straight table with a grey background

   b. Add the fields that are NOT associated with your current selection

2. Place it next to your main charts, not in a separate tab

   a. Context is everything here

3. Look at it every time you think you are done analyzing

Most of us know the feeling of finding an answer and closing the app. But think
about it: how many questions did you never ask, because the data looked fine?
We don't know what we don't know.

This simple grey panel got me thinking in a different way. It is the tip of an
iceberg and now I can at least see how big the iceberg is.

I'm curious to know if and how you are using this concept. If you are not, go
ahead and experiment with it.

Have fun and happy designing!
